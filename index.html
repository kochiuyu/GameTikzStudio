🌳 Sequential Moves (Turns)

Players choose one after another (e.g., Player 1 moves, then Player 2 reacts, or Nature flips a coin).

Best for: Market entry deterrence, bargaining, chess/poker turns, backward induction, subgame perfection.
Use Game Tree Studio →
📊 Simultaneous Moves (At Once)

Both players choose their actions at the same time without knowing what the other picked.

Best for: Prisoner's dilemma, rock-paper-scissors, finding pure/mixed Nash equilibria, dominated strategies.

Game Theory Jargon Buster (Key Terms Explained in Plain English)
Payoff $(u_1, u_2)$: The reward or score. Crucial rule: The 1st number is Player 1's score; the 2nd number is Player 2's score.
Nash Equilibrium: A stable outcome where no player regrets their choice given what the other player picked.
Information Set: Dashed line connecting tree nodes when a player doesn't know which previous move was made.
Backward Induction: Solving a game from the end back to the start to find the rational path (Subgame Perfect Equilibrium).
